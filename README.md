One word for your Wi-Fi: **Ghosting**. This program scores candidate words against the brief's five criteria — on-subject, funny at once, true to home Wi-Fi, a fresh angle, instantly understood — picks the winner, and writes it to `entry.txt`. The joke is the word itself: home Wi-Fi that vanishes mid-call, like a bad date who stops replying.

## Why Ghosting

- On-subject: it describes Wi-Fi as lived — there one minute, gone the next.
- Funny at once: everyone knows the feeling of being ghosted; no explaining needed.
- True: the dropout mid-call, mid-game, mid-download is the universal home Wi-Fi experience.
- Fresh: it dodges the words everyone reaches for first — slow, laggy, buffering.
- Instant: one familiar word, spelled the way everyone already spells it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
